Privacy Policy for Arrow Puzzle

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Arrow Puzzle ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal information is collected, used, and disclosed by Arrow Puzzle.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our game, we may collect certain information automatically or through your voluntary actions:

  • Account Information: If you choose to sign in using Google Sign-In, we collect your name, email address, and profile picture to sync your game progress across devices and display your profile on leaderboards.
  • Game Data: Your level progress, scores, achievements, and custom levels are saved locally and optionally synced securely to Google Firebase Firestore.
  • Purchases & Subscriptions: In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed securely through Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your credit card or payment details.
  • Device and Usage Data: We use Google Firebase Analytics, Firebase App Check, and Google AdMob to collect anonymous analytics, crash diagnostics, app integrity signals, and ad interaction data to improve gameplay stability and deliver personalized or non-personalized advertisements.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and improve our game features (leaderboards, cloud save, community levels).
  • To manage and verify in-app purchases and subscriptions via Google Play Billing.
  • To display advertisements via Google AdMob and analyze gameplay metrics.
  • To respond to support inquiries and protect the game against fraud and abuse.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app integrates with trusted third-party services that have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services & AdMob
  • Google Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Analytics, App Check)

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures (including HTTPS encryption, Firebase App Check, and Firebase Security Rules) to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your account and cloud-synced game data only for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide the service. You may request deletion of your account and associated cloud data at any time by contacting us at kongonamongo@gmail.com. Local game data can be removed at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

6. Children's Privacy

Our game is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page with an updated effective date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or data deletion requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: kongonamongo@gmail.com
